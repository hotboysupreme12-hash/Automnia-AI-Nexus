# Model provider logos

These local 512×512 PNG assets are used by the model catalog picker. The provider marks are hosted by the Noosphere provider-logo registry:

`https://blockchainstarter.nyc3.digitaloceanspaces.com/noosphere/logos/png/`

The LM Studio mark is rasterized locally from the Simple Icons CDN:

`https://cdn.simpleicons.org/lmstudio`

Provider names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. The assets are bundled locally so the picker remains available offline after installation.
